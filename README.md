# I'm Somil Aggarwal

I'm a tinkerer and a game fanatic. My interests have taken me all over the place, but I'm ultimately motivated by using technology to solve interesting, people-oriented problems.

Throughout my career thus far, this has manifested in building [a data tracking tool for farmers](https://somilagg123.github.io/agcess/), [a dating app for friends to match their friends](https://somilagg123.github.io/mutual/), and [a social platform for watch enthusiasts](https://somilagg123.github.io/horlo/). They're now preserved in a little digital museum of past projects.

Now, I've turned my attention to AI and how it interfaces with people. I joined Decagon AI to learn how to build and deploy production AI agents at scale.

However, like I said, I'm always tinkering. On the side, I am building [better knowledge factories for agents](https://customermemory.dev/). I often spend mornings reading, my penchant for which has resurfaced with the rise of [awesome technical blogs](https://somilaggarwal.com/#library).

Outside of work is where I get to invest in being a huge game fanatic: board games, strategy games, party games, you name it. I'm tinkering with AI to build [awesome custom-made games](https://forhappyhour.com/) for my friends.
